# Airline-Passenger-Satisfaction-Analysis


1. Business Request

  To identify key factors influencing customer satisfaction in the airline industry and provide actionable recommendations to improve service quality, customer experience, and loyalty.

2. Main Idea

  Analyze a dataset of over 116,000 airline passengers to understand customer demographics, service ratings, flight delays, and ticket classes. 
  
  The goal is to uncover patterns in satisfaction levels, compare perceptions between satisfied and unsatisfied customers, and evaluate how service aspects and delays affect overall experience.

3. Tools and Techniques Used

  Tools: Power BI (mainly using DAX and Power Query for loading and presenting key metrics).
  
  Techniques:
- DAX Measures.
- Dynamic Titles & Dynamic Measures.
- Histogram Analysis.
- Outlier Detection.
- Ranking service factors based on passenger ratings.
- Bubble Charts (used to find relationships between numerical variables) and Key Influencer Visuals (used to identify the top 4 factors that have the most impact on customer satisfaction).
