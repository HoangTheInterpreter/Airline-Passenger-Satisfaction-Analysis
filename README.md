# Airline-Passenger-Satisfaction-Analysis


1. Business Request

  To identify key factors influencing customer satisfaction in the airline industry and provide actionable recommendations to improve service quality, customer experience, and loyalty.

2. Main Idea

  Analyze a dataset of over 116,000 airline passengers to understand customer demographics, service ratings, flight delays, and ticket classes. And here are the variables: 
  
  Target Variable:
  
    Satisfaction: Overall passenger satisfaction level (Satisfied / Unsatisfied)
  
  Demographic Variables:
    
    Gender: Male or Female
    
    CustomerType: Loyal or Disloyal customer
    
    Age: Passenger’s age
    
    TypeofTravel: Business or Personal travel
    
    Class: Ticket class (Economy, Premium Economy, Business)
  
  Flight Details:
    
    FlightDistance: Distance of the flight in miles
    
    DepartureDelayinMinutes: Delay time at departure
    
    ArrivalDelayinMinutes: Delay time at arrival

    Seatcomfort: Passenger’s rating of seat comfort during the flight.

  Service Rating:
  
    Departure/Arrivaltimeconvenient: How convenient the departure and arrival times were for the passenger.

    Foodanddrink: Quality and satisfaction with meals and beverages served on board.

    Gatelocation: Convenience of the boarding gate location from the passenger’s perspective.

    Inflightwifiservice: Passenger’s rating of the Wi-Fi service provided during the flight.

    Inflightentertainment: Satisfaction with entertainment options available during the flight.

    Onlinesupport: Rating of the airline’s online customer support services.

    EaseofOnlinebooking: How easy and smooth the online ticket booking process was.

    On-boardservice: Overall service experience provided by the cabin crew during the flight.

    Legroomservice: Passenger’s comfort level regarding legroom space.

    Baggagehandling: Satisfaction with how baggage was managed and delivered.

    Checkinservice: Passenger’s experience with the check-in process at the airport.

    Cleanliness: Cleanliness of the cabin and onboard facilities.

    Onlineboarding: Ease and efficiency of the online boarding process.
  
  The goal is to uncover patterns in satisfaction levels, compare perceptions between satisfied and unsatisfied customers, and evaluate how service aspects and delays affect overall experience.

3. Tools and Techniques Used

  Tools: Power BI (mainly using DAX and Power Query for loading and presenting key metrics).
  
  Techniques:
- DAX Measures.
- Dynamic Titles & Dynamic Measures.
- Histogram Analysis.
- Outlier Detection.
- Ranking service factors based on passenger ratings.
- Bubble Charts (used to find relationships between numerical variables) and Key Influencer Visuals (used to identify the top 4 factors that have the most impact on customer satisfaction).
